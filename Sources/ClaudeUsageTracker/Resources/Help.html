Claude Usage Tracker

A floating panel that keeps your Claude.ai usage limits in view.

The floating panel

Each row is one usage window:

  • Session — your rolling 5-hour limit (all models).
  • Weekly — your overall 7-day limit across all models combined (Opus, Sonnet and Haiku).
  • Weekly (Opus) — a separate weekly cap that applies to Opus only; appears when Opus tracking is enabled (see Additional Tracking).
  • Weekly (Fable) — appears when Fable tracking is enabled (see Additional Tracking).

Reading a bar

  • The filled bar and the percentage show how much of that window you've used.
  • The thin vertical marker shows how far you are through the window time-wise — if the fill is behind the marker, you're pacing comfortably; ahead of it, you're using faster than the clock.
  • The “resets in …” pill counts down to when that window refreshes.
  • Colour: bars use your chosen colour in the normal range and switch to amber from 75% and red from 90%, so warnings always stand out.

Menu bar & “Always on top”

Use the Always on top switch in the panel header to keep the panel floating above other windows. When it's off, the panel hides and you summon it by clicking the menu-bar icon. Left-click the icon to show/hide the panel; right-click it for Settings and Quit.

Settings

Open Settings from the gear in the panel footer, or the menu-bar icon's right-click menu. It has five sections:

Connect

Click Sign in to Claude.ai to connect your account so the app can read your usage — nothing is sent anywhere else. The pill shows whether you're connected, and Sign out of Claude disconnects. Advanced users can paste a sessionKey value manually.

Additional Tracking

  • Track API spend — enable and paste an Admin API key (sk-ant-admin…) to show your month-to-date API spend in the panel.
  • Track Opus weekly usage — adds a Weekly (Opus) row for the separate weekly cap that applies to Opus only (it also still counts toward your overall Weekly).
  • Track Fable weekly usage — adds a Weekly (Fable) row mirroring the per-model Fable limit shown in the Claude app.

General

  • Menu bar shows — choose whether the menu-bar percentage reflects your Session or Weekly limit.
  • Refresh interval — how often usage is fetched (10–300 s).
  • Threshold notifications — get notified when a window crosses 75 / 90 / 95%.
  • Launch at login — start the app automatically when you log in.

Appearance

  • Always on top — keep the panel above other windows (or hide it until summoned).
  • Bar colour — pick the normal-range colour for the bars: Green, Claude, or Blue. Amber and red still appear as usage gets high. The Settings accent follows your choice.
  • Show menu bar icon — toggle the menu-bar icon (kept on while “Always on top” is off, so the panel stays reachable).
  • Appearance mode — System, Light, or Dark for the whole app.
  • Panel opacity — make the floating panel more see-through.

About & Help

Shows the app version, this help guide (Open Help), a link to the latest online guide, and the CramerLabs site.

Status

The coloured dot in the panel footer reflects Claude's service status. Click it to open status.claude.com.
